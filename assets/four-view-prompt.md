# Character sheet prompt template

Create ONE professional photorealistic character consistency sheet, landscape16:9. Depict the SAME SINGLE character: [IDENTITY, AGE, FACE, HAIR, BUILD]. Keep [EXACT COSTUME, SHOES, ACCESSORIES] identical in all views. Plain neutral studio background, even illumination, detailed skin and cloth.

Left approximately one third: eye-level frontal close-up, complete hair top to collarbones, neutral expression, hair silhouette contained with margin. Right approximately two thirds: equal-scale full-body front, exact90-degree anatomical left profile facing image left, and rear. Head tops and soles aligned; full bodies visible. Relaxed arms or the user's A-pose, feet together. Hands empty unless the design requires a prop. No text, panels or extra identities.

The four depictions show one character from different angles, not four people to insert into the video.
